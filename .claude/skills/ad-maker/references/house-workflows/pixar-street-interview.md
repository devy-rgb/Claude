Myoguard Pro — Pixar Street Interview Ad
Production Workflow
1. Concept
Product: Myoguard Pro, a muscle-preservation supplement for GLP-1 users (creatine, Ca-HMB, zinc carnosine, 80+ vitamins and minerals).
Format: Pixar-style 3D animated street interview. A golden retriever plays an eager interviewer; a grey tabby cat, "Dr. Whiskers," plays the deadpan expert, wearing a white doctor's lab coat.
Tone: fast-paced, comedic, deadpan-vs-eager contrast between the two characters.
Voice direction: Dog — young, cheerful male voice, American accent, upbeat. Cat — heavy, middle-aged, authoritative male voice, dry deadpan delivery.
Staging: a single locked reference frame is used for the entire video. Both characters stand side by side with a slight gap between them, the dog holding a toy microphone between the two of them. No camera movement across any clip — every clip uses the exact same framing, angle, and composition as the reference image. Characters keep eye contact with each other, never the camera.
Runtime target: under 60 seconds. Actual total across all 7 clips: roughly 51-52 seconds.
Animation tool: Google Omni (Gemini Omni) — generates video, dialogue, and audio together per clip. Two technical constraints shaped the whole plan: a roughly 10-second cap per clip, and no memory between clips, so every clip's prompt restates the full identity, environment, and camera locks from scratch.
2. Script
Total spoken content: ~120 words across the two characters, timed to land at a fast conversational pace under the 60-second cap.
