MYOGUARD PRO
“The Nurse's Secret” — POV AI UGC Video Ad
Full Production Workflow: Script, Framework, Stills & Video Prompts
Production tools: Nanobanana Pro (start/end frames) → Google Omni (video generation)

1. Concept Overview
Product: Myoguard Pro — a supplement for GLP-1 users, positioned around preventing muscle loss during weight loss. Contains creatine, Ca-HMB, zinc carnosine, and 80+ vitamins and minerals.
Concept: A POV-style video shot from the patient's own eyes, lying in a hospital bed. A nurse enters the room, closes the door behind her, and reveals “a secret the doctor won't tell him” — that GLP-1 weight loss burns muscle along with fat — before introducing Myoguard Pro as the fix.
Framework: Industry Contrarian — DISRUPT. The whispered, door-checking “secret” device delivers the framework's “declassify the secret” beat and turns the exposed truth into something that feels intercepted rather than advertised.
Format: 6 clips, ~50–55 seconds total. Produced as Nanobanana Pro start/end still frames → animated and voiced with Google Omni (native audio, ~10s cap per clip).
Character: Nurse — no user-supplied reference photo; generated fresh. Glam, above-average attractive features, professional standard-issue navy scrubs (kept non-revealing/non-sexualized wardrobe). Warm, genuine Southern American accent, locked and reused verbatim across every clip.
Compliance: An on-screen disclosure (“Dramatization for illustrative purposes. Actor, not a licensed medical professional. Consult your doctor before starting any supplement.”) must be burned in during CapCut post-production, since Omni cannot render on-screen text.

2. Locked Script — DISRUPT Framework
Framework: Industry Contrarian — D.I.S.R.U.P.T. The nurse speaks directly to the patient throughout (“you,” never generic “people”). The video opens with her entering the room — not mid-scene.

5 Hooks (locked opener + 4 alternates)
1. “Hey... I have to tell you something that doctors here won't say.” (LOCKED)  |  on-screen: She waited for the door to close, then said this.
2. “Hey, before anyone else comes in — I need to tell you something.”  |  on-screen: She said this before anyone could stop her.
3. “You're not going to hear this from your doctor, so I'm telling you.”  |  on-screen: What your doctor isn't telling you about your GLP-1.
4. “I probably shouldn't say this, but you deserve to know.”  |  on-screen: The secret nobody's told you before you keep losing weight.
5. “Quick, before someone walks in — there's something about your GLP-1 nobody's told you.”  |  on-screen: She checked the hallway first, then said this.

CTA: “Ask about Myoguard Pro today” — reinforces the core benefit (keep the muscle, lose the fat). No stored risk-reversal / guarantee offer yet — confirm before finalizing if one should be folded into the closing line.
