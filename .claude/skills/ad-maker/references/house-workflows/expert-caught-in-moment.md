Follivé Salon UGC Ad
Complete Production Workflow — Concept, Script, Reference Images & Video Clip Prompts
1. Concept Overview
A UGC-style video ad: a woman is getting her hair done at an upscale salon when her hairdresser notices early signs of thinning at her crown mid-appointment, points it out, and recommends a hair-growth gummy. The scene plays out as a real, candid salon moment rather than a scripted testimonial — the stylist's authority (someone who handles hundreds of heads of hair) is what makes the recommendation land.
Framework used: PAS (Pain → Agitate → Solution), delivered through a trusted third-party expert rather than a direct-to-camera testimonial.
2. Brand & Characters
Brand
Follivé — a hypothetical hair-growth supplement, delivered as a gummy (two per day).
Marco — the stylist
30 years old, Hispanic, short bleached-blonde hair, black-framed glasses, small gold hoop earrings, slightly fuller/puffy face shape, warm tan-olive skin tone, light stubble. Wears a fitted plain black shirt. Personality: warm, animated, expressive, higher-energy by default, quick with a compliment and quicker with the truth — calls clients "babe" or "honey." Theatrical and playful by default; the concern in this ad's hook moment is genuine, not a bit.
The Client
Woman in her late twenties, brunette, shoulder-length hair, fair-medium skin tone, average-pretty natural features, no heavy glam. Seated in the styling chair throughout, wearing a black salon cape with faint "STUDIO" lettering.
Setting
An upscale designer hair studio — marble-topped styling station, brushed-brass cart, glass product bottles with minimalist labels, large brass-framed mirror, warm Edison-bulb pendant lighting, floating marble shelves, potted greenery, cream-leather styling chairs.
3. Ad Script — PAS Framework

5 Hooks (same body, swap the opener)
1. "Okay wait — babe, how long has your part been this wide?" | on-screen: She noticed before I did
2. "My hairdresser stopped mid-appointment to tell me this." | on-screen: This wasn't in the plan today
3. MARCO: "I see this in like 1 out of every 3 clients now." | on-screen: The thing stylists keep spotting
4. "I thought I was just shedding a little more. My stylist said no." | on-screen: It wasn't 'just shedding'
5. "He paused, put down the brush, and went 'okay, can I be honest with you?'" | on-screen: The mirror doesn't lie

CTA: Get Follivé (link in caption/bio) + risk-reversal — "money-back if you don't see visible regrowth in 90 days."
