AGENCYOS — Hot Seat Ad
Production Workflow Document — DFY AI Agency Offer, Crowd Q&A / Hot Seat Format
1. Concept & Format Analysis
Source material: two uploaded conference-style clips plus reference screenshots. Format identified: real live-event footage repurposed as short-form social proof — a crowd member stands with a mic and states a specific result or problem, and the coach on stage responds with a short, tactical, quotable answer. Karaoke-style burned-in captions, jump cuts on each new phrase, roomy live-mic audio, no polish.
What makes the format work
Specificity over polish — real dollar figures and operational detail sell harder than production value.
The crowd member does the objection-raising; the coach never hedges.
Two consistent characters, two consistent backgrounds — audience seating vs. stage backdrop.
Captions are short, word-synced, centered lower-third — reads as real event captions, not ad subtitles.
Offer & mechanism
Product: the DFY AI agency offer. Mechanism used in this ad: AGENCYOS builds the AI tools/systems; the buyer handles sales and client delivery. Proof point featured: a specific revenue result in a short timeframe ($14K in 90 days).
Framework applied: HOT SEAT (Crowd + Coach) — blends the Bandwagon/Crowd angle (a live crowd already validating the model) with the Founder-Objections/PROVE angle (a real, checkable number crushing skepticism).
2. Final Script

Voice casting: Crowd Member — young British male, standard South-East English accent. Coach — adult male, New York accent, calm and composed delivery (not fast-talking).
